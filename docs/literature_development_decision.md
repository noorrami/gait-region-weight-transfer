Direct chapter access failed. A similarly titled chapter also appears in the book; do not mix its authors/pages with Sun et al.

Prior reviewed 2017 fixed decision-fusion weights already motivate the NM-selected control. Neither fixed-weight reuse nor regional fusion is a novelty claim. ## Decision and gate meaning

The comparison set and transfer estimand are sufficiently specified to run a controlled empirical study without claiming an original weighting algorithm. Mark the development literature-review gate assessed with limitations, replacing the earlier pending state. This gate means that known overlap, evidence gaps and claim restrictions have been reviewed and recorded. It does not mean all full texts were obtained, that the exact experiment has never appeared, or that publication novelty is established.

Freeze the experimental design and conduct development. Keep novelty status **unproven**, continue literature work before manuscript novelty claims, and retain full-text/forward-citation limitations. No additional method, view, preprocessing threshold or weight grid is selected from future coat results. If later literature shows exact overlap, report it and revise the contribution transparently; do not alter the primary experiment to chase novelty after test inspection.

This dated decision supersedes its blanket instruction to defer all plan freezing, while preserving its unresolved source-access facts and restrictions on novelty claims.
